# 📊 Excel Sales & Finance Analytics Project | AtliQ Hardwares

## 📝 Project Overview

AtliQ Hardwares is a global hardware company specializing in PCs, printers, mice, and computer accessories. The company faced challenges in optimizing sales performance and improving net gross margins across international markets.

This project focuses on analyzing **500,000+ sales records** using Microsoft Excel to transform raw and unorganized data into actionable business insights. Through extensive ETL processes, data modeling, and reporting, the project aims to support strategic decision-making and KPI tracking for both Sales and Finance domains.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

The organization required a data-driven solution to:

* Monitor sales performance across different markets
* Compare actual sales against targets
* Analyze customer contribution and profitability
* Improve financial planning and forecasting
* Enhance decision-making using interactive reports

---

# 📈 Sales Analytics

## 📌 Objectives

* Develop a **Customer Performance Report**
* Create a **Market Performance vs Sales Target Report**

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Identified top-performing customers and high-revenue markets
* Analyzed sales trends and critical KPIs
* Compared market sales against predefined targets
* Supported customer discount optimization and negotiation strategies
* Identified potential international business expansion opportunities

---

# 💰 Finance Analytics

## 📌 Objectives

Build Profit & Loss (P&L) reports by:

* Fiscal Year
* Months
* Markets

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Evaluated financial performance across multiple business regions
* Supported budgeting and forecasting processes
* Enabled benchmarking against previous periods and industry standards
* Improved stakeholder communication through insightful financial reports
